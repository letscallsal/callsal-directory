Excalidraw is a hand-drawn style whiteboard that runs in the browser. Official copy on the Plus site: Free and open source. No account is needed. Just start drawing. The app at excalidraw.com autosaves to browser storage and warns you to save a file, because that storage can be cleared.

It is for the person who needs a diagram, a wireframe, or a meeting sketch and does not want to open Figma. GitHub lists an infinite canvas, shape libraries, export to PNG, SVG, clipboard, and an .excalidraw file. The hosted app adds a PWA, real-time collaboration, end-to-end encryption, and shareable read-only links.

The editor is free forever. Official pricing lists full editor functions, one infinite scene, unlimited collaborators, libraries, and file export. Excalidraw Plus is listed at 6 dollars a month per user, with a 14-day trial, for unlimited cloud scenes, access management, presentations, and team features. Generative AI exists on both. Plus calls the free side limited.

Use the free site when the drawing is a one-off. Skip Plus if you are fine exporting a file. Skip the whole thing if you already live in a full design tool and need pixels, not a sketch.
